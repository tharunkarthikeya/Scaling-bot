# Adira Enterprises logo assets

- `adira-logo-light.svg`: transparent asset for white and light backgrounds.
- `adira-logo-dark.svg`: transparent white asset for navy, black, and dark backgrounds.
- `adira-logo-light.png` and `adira-logo-dark.png`: transparent 1180 × 280 PNG exports.
- `adira-logo-light-preview.png` and `adira-logo-dark-preview.png`: 1280 × 360 presentation previews.

## Brand colors

- Navy: `#00265F`
- Crimson: `#BF0037`
- Dark background: `#071426` to `#001A43`

Keep clear space around the lockup equal to at least half the symbol's circular cutout. Do not stretch the logo, change the color split, or place the light version on a dark background.
